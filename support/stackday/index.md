## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I add a supplement?**
Tap the "+" button in My Stack. Enter the supplement name, dosage, and when you take it (morning, afternoon, evening). StackDay adds it to your daily checklist.

**How do streaks work?**
Each day you log all your supplements, your streak increases by one. Miss a day, and your streak resets. Streaks help you build and maintain a consistent supplement habit.

**Can I track supplements I take at different times of day?**
Yes! Assign each supplement to a time of day (your configured daily slots). Your Dashboard groups supplements by time so you can check them off throughout the day.

**Is my supplement data private?**
Yes. Primary records are stored on your device, but optional AI/scanning tools send relevant input to online providers, and analytics, diagnostics and subscriptions process service data. Usage events can include supplement names; error reports may include app screenshots. See the [Privacy Policy](https://foggy-labs.github.io/privacy/stackday/) for details. Use manual entry if you do not want optional AI processing.

**How do I manage my subscription?**
Go to your iPhone Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions > StackDay. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from there. You can also manage your subscription from the Settings tab within the app.

**How do I restore my purchases?**
Open StackDay > Settings > Restore Purchases. This will restore any active subscriptions associated with your Apple ID.

**What happens if I delete the app?**
Deleting the app removes its local records. Before deleting it, create a full backup in Settings → Backup & Restore and save it to a safe Files destination. You can restore a full backup in that screen. Exported files and service-provider records are not erased by deleting the app; see the Privacy Policy for deletion requests.

**I found a bug. How do I report it?**
Email us at npc@foggylabs.ai with a description of the issue, your device model, and iOS version. Screenshots help!
