# StackDay — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 23, 2026**

StackDay is operated by Fog Hill LLC ("we", "us"). This policy describes on-device records, optional online features, subscriptions, diagnostics and marketing measurement. Contact [npc@foggylabs.ai](mailto:npc@foggylabs.ai) with privacy questions or requests.

## Your tracking records

StackDay stores its primary supplement, medication, peptide, dose-history, weight and wellness records on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. An account is not required. Local storage does **not** mean that every piece of information stays on your device: online features, analytics, diagnostics and subscription services transmit the information described below.

Backups and exports are created when you choose to save or share them. Files saved to iCloud Drive or another provider are also subject to that provider's practices. Backups can contain sensitive health information; choose their destination and recipients carefully. Deleting the app does not delete copies you previously exported or shared.

## Optional scanner and AI features

When you use label scanning, Apple's Vision framework recognizes text on your device. The recognized label text and a subscription-service user identifier are sent to our Cloudflare-hosted service for ingredient parsing. This route sends recognized text, rather than the original camera image, to the AI service.

When you use the AI Supplement Advisor, your submitted message, recent conversation context, and active supplement names are sent to our service to generate a response. Other optional AI analysis tools, when available and used, may send relevant regimen details and findings for the requested analysis. Do not enter names, contact details or other information you do not want processed by these services.

Our AI routes use **Cloudflare**, **OpenRouter** and an **Anthropic** model. These providers process the submitted information to deliver the response and may process service, security or abuse-prevention data under their respective terms and settings. We do not promise that third-party AI processing is exclusively on-device, that providers never retain inputs, or that a particular retention/training setting applies unless it has been verified for the service in use. You can avoid this processing by not using the optional online features and using manual entry instead.

## Analytics, diagnostics and identifiers

We use **PostHog** for product analytics and **Sentry** for crash and performance diagnostics. Events may include app screens, features used, onboarding answers, schedule interactions, supplement or medication names entered or logged, usage times, subscription interactions, app/device information and pseudonymous identifiers. These events can reveal information about your health-related routine.

Diagnostic reports can include errors, performance traces, device/app context, navigation breadcrumbs and screenshots of the app at the time of an error. Those screenshots may include information visible on the screen, including tracking or health information. Do not assume diagnostic data is free of user content.

Pseudonymous identifiers can link events from the same installation or subscription user. They are not the same as fully anonymous data. Network services receive IP addresses to handle requests; analytics/attribution services may infer an approximate region from them. StackDay does not request GPS location or precise-location access.

## Subscriptions and advertising measurement

**Apple** processes purchases and payment details through the App Store. **RevenueCat** processes subscription identifiers, purchase history, receipts/transaction information and entitlement status so the app can provide paid features. Our service may keep subscription-access, security and offer records associated with the subscription-service identifier. We do not receive your payment card number from App Store purchases.

**AppsFlyer** and **TikTok Business SDK** support install attribution and advertising/conversion measurement. Shared information can include permitted device identifiers, pseudonymous user identifiers, campaign/referral information, app launches, onboarding/paywall events, and trial/purchase conversion events. Identifiers may be linked between analytics, attribution and subscription services. Where required, cross-app tracking and access to the advertising identifier require Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission. This policy does not claim that declining that prompt disables all product analytics or essential service processing.

## Apple Health, camera and photos

Apple Health integration is optional and uses the categories you authorize, such as sleep, steps and workouts. The integration reads data for in-app displays and comparisons. This permission is separate from permission for advertising tracking. Do not intentionally send Apple Health records to the AI advisor or to advertising services. Information displayed in the app may appear in diagnostic screenshots as described above.

Camera or photo-library access is requested for features such as label scanning when you choose to use them. You can deny or revoke those permissions in iOS Settings and use manual entry. We do not access your contacts or microphone as part of the tracking and scanning features described here.

Information submitted to support includes your message and any attachments or contact information you choose to send. We do not sell your personal tracking records as a standalone dataset.

## Retention, security and transfers

Local records remain until you edit/delete them, restore a backup, or remove the app. Exported backups remain at the destinations you choose. Service, analytics, attribution, purchase and diagnostic data can remain after app deletion, subject to the purpose of processing, provider configuration, security needs and legal obligations. Contact us to request information about retention or deletion for your data; reinstalling or deleting the app is not a request to erase provider-held records.

We use platform protections and encrypted network transport for supported services. No storage or transmission method is perfectly secure. Providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where applicable, we rely on contractual and other safeguards for international transfers.

## Your choices and rights

- Use manual tracking instead of optional online AI/scanning tools.
- Manage Apple Health, camera, photos and tracking permissions in iOS Settings.
- Manage or cancel subscriptions in your [Apple account](https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions).
- Delete local records or the app, and separately delete exported files when desired.
- Contact [npc@foggylabs.ai](mailto:npc@foggylabs.ai) to request access, correction, deletion or information about service-held data. We may need information to locate the relevant pseudonymous identifier or purchase record; do not email a full health backup unless needed and requested.

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction or objection, and to complain to a data protection authority. We respond in accordance with applicable law. We do not treat refusal of advertising tracking as refusal of all essential subscription or service processing.

StackDay is not directed to children under 13. The App Store age rating and applicable local requirements may impose a higher minimum. Contact us if you believe a child has supplied personal information to our online services.
